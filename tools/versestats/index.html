Understanding Website Metrics & Traffic

Key Metrics

  • Pages Per Visit: Average number of pages a visitor views per session. 2.5 – 4+ is healthy; higher means better engagement.
  • Bounce Rate: Percentage of visitors who leave after viewing only one page. 40% – 50% is ideal; lower indicates strong content or user retention.
  • Visit Duration: Average time a visitor spends on your site per session. 2 – 4 minutes is healthy; longer times indicate good engagement.
  • Returning Visitors: Ratio of repeat visitors compared to new ones. ≥1.3 indicates loyal users; lower may signal low retention.
  • Bot Detection: Indicators of automated traffic: very low pages/visit (<1.2), extremely short sessions (<0.5 min), or unknown/empty traffic sources.

How We Spot Human, Bot, or Suspicious Traffic

  • Pages Per Visit: ≥2.5 → Likely Human, <1.2 → Likely Bot
  • Bounce Rate: 40–50% → Healthy, >80% → Bot or Suspicious
  • Visit Duration: 2–4 min → Human engagement, <0.5 min → Bot
  • Engagement Ratio: <1.05 → Bot, 1.05–1.7 → Suspicious, >1.7 → Human (calculated as events ÷ visitors, reflects interaction level)
  • Returning Visitors / Traffic Sources: Loyal / Known Sources → Human, Low returning or unknown sources → Suspicious/Bot

Summary for Improvement

  • Human: Good engagement, normal session time, and consistent visitor behavior.
  • Suspicious: Some low engagement, short sessions, or minor anomalies. Monitor and optimize content or traffic sources.
  • Bot: Very low engagement, extremely short sessions, or unknown sources — likely automated traffic. Consider adding bot protection or filtering.

Focus on improving Pages Per Visit, lowering Bounce Rate, increasing Visit Duration, and attracting real returning users. This will help convert Suspicious traffic into Human traffic and grow quality engagement.
