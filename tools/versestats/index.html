Understanding Website Metrics & Traffic

Key Metrics

  • Pages Per Visit: 2.5 – 4+ (higher is better)
  • Bounce Rate: 40% – 50% ideal
  • Visit Duration: 2 – 4 minutes is healthy
  • Returning Visitors: Visit ratio ≥ 1.3 indicates loyalty
  • Bot Detection: Pages/visitor <1.2, very short duration, or unknown sources may indicate bot traffic

How We Spot Human, Bot, or Suspicious Traffic

  • Pages Per Visit: High engagement (≥2.5) → Human, Low (<1.2) → Bot
  • Bounce Rate: 40–50% healthy, >80% → Bot/suspicious
  • Visit Duration: 2–4 min → Human, <0.5 min → Bot
  • Engagement Ratio: Ratio <1.05 → Bot, 1.05–1.7 → Suspicious, >1.7 → Human
  • Returning Visitors / Unknown Sources: Loyal → Human, Low returning → Suspicious/Bot

Summary for Improvement

  • Human: Good engagement, normal session time, consistent visitor behavior.
  • Suspicious: Some low engagement, short duration, or small anomalies — monitor closely.
  • Bot: Very low engagement, extremely short sessions, or unknown sources — likely automated traffic.

Focus on improving Pages Per Visit, lowering Bounce Rate, increasing session duration, and attracting real returning users to convert Suspicious traffic into Human traffic.
