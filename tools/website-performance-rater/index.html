Understanding Website Metrics & Traffic

  • Pages Per Visit: 2.5 – 4+ (higher is better)
  • Bounce Rate: 40% – 50% ideal
  • Visit Duration: 2 – 4 minutes is healthy
  • Returning Visitors: Visit ratio ≥ 1.3 indicates loyalty
  • Bot Detection: Pages/visitor <1.2, very short duration, or unknown sources may indicate bot traffic

Use the Performance Rater to improve engagement, and the Bot Detector to check for suspicious traffic patterns. Both tools together give a full picture of your website health.
