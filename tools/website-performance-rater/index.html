Understanding Your Metrics (2025–2026 Benchmarks)

Benchmarks vary widely by industry (e.g., e-commerce often has lower bounce rates and higher pages/visit than blogs or B2B SaaS). These are cross-industry medians/averages from recent reports—use them as general guides, not strict rules.

  • Unique Monthly Visitors: Number of distinct users in a month.
    Strong: 10,000+ (many small/medium sites: 1k–15k median; scale to your niche)
  • Total Visits: Includes repeats.
    Strong: Returning ratio ≥1.3–1.5× (loyalty indicator)
  • Page Views: Total pages viewed monthly.
    Strong: 20,000+ (highly variable by traffic volume)
  • Pages per Visit: Pages viewed per session.
    Strong: ≥3–4 | Average: ~2.8 cross-industry | Excellent: 6–10+ (e.g., e-commerce)
  • Bounce Rate: % single-page sessions.
    Strong: ≤40–45% | Average: 44–50% | High (needs work): >60% (e-commerce often 25–40%, blogs 50–70%+)
  • Visit Duration: Average time per session.
    Strong: ≥2–3 min | Average: ~2:00–2:20 min | Excellent: 3–8+ min (content sites longer)

Tip: Improve one area at a time—better content often boosts pages/visit, duration, and lowers bounce. Always compare to your industry and traffic sources (e.g., organic vs. paid).
