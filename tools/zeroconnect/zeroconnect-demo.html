DEMO MODE, simulated verification

How it works

1

Customer enters amount + wallet

They type how much USDT they want to pay and the address they'll send from.

2

They send USDT normally

Copy the receiver address (or scan QR) and send from any wallet, Phantom, MetaMask, etc.

3

Click "I paid, Verify"

ZeroConnect checks the blockchain for a matching transfer in the last 45 minutes.

4

Redirect on success

Customer is sent to your success URL with tx hash, amount, and chain as query params.

This is a demo. Click "Simulate successful payment" to experience the full flow without sending real USDT.
