SnappQuest Submission Game

Learn how to submit a quest in 2 minutes – play & earn knowledge!

1 Complete the quest

Every quest has clear instructions — like:

  • Follow
  • Retweet
  • Comment
  • join Telegram, etc.

Do the task first — then come back to submit proof on SnappQuest.

Example task: "Retweet this post and tag 2 friends"

→ You already retweeted and tagged @friend1 @friend2
2 Submit proof

You must give the sponsor a way to verify you really did the task.

Most common proofs:

  • Your X / Twitter profile link
  • Direct link to your retweet / reply / comment
  • Screenshot (upload to imgur / free image host → paste link)
3 Fill the submission form

Paste links exactly like this in the SnappQuest submit modal:

https://x.com/yourusername/status/1234567890123456789 https://x.com/yourusername https://imgur.com/a/your-proof-screenshot
